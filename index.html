Move to Burn It

Diet sets the calorie deficit — training decides whether you lose fat or muscle. To keep the muscle your protein is protecting:

🏋️Lift weights 3–4×/week. The single strongest signal to hold onto muscle while cutting.
🚶Walk 8,000–12,000 steps a day. The easiest, most sustainable calorie burn there is.
❤️2–3 sport or cardio sessions you enjoy. You'll only keep what you actually like doing.
🔥Add 1–2 short HIIT sessions once the habit sticks, for extra burn in less time.
